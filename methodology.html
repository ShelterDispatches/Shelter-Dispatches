Repair Typology Reference
Light Repair

Thermal Envelope Only: Windows, Doors, Roof

The core Light Repair scope: energy-saving windows, entrance doors, roofing panels, drainage and slopes. No structural works. Average cost: $2,116–$3,702 (private house).

SOP Table 3.1Cat. 1
Medium Repair

Full Envelope + Minimum Habitability

All Light Repair elements plus partial/full roof structure, partition repairs, minor plumbing/electrical, sanitary fixtures. Average cost: $4,924–$8,611 (private house).

SOP Table 3.2Cat. 1
Hard Repair

Structural Intervention — Category 2

Load-bearing wall, foundation, or floor plate damage. Requires engineering assessment, project design, and Framework Agreement with contractor. Budget: highly variable, typically $15,000–$80,000+.
