Hard Repairs at Scale: Why Category 2 Can't Wait Any Longer

Three years into the response, the easy work is done. Light and medium repairs have been delivered to tens of thousands of households — and the need for them is visibly declining. What remains is harder, more expensive, and structurally more complex. Category 2 demand is accelerating, driven by new damage and a growing backlog of households that were assessed, found ineligible for Cat. 1, and have been waiting ever since.
