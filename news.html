Winter Preparedness UN Ukraine 22 Jul 2026 ukraine.un.org
UN Ukraine: $358.4 Million Needed to Protect 2.1 Million People Through Winter 2026–27

The UN Ukraine Winter Preparedness and Response Plan 2026–2027 calls for $358.4 million to support 2.1 million of the most vulnerable people across the period October 2026 – March 2027. The plan spans 13 humanitarian clusters and prioritises shelter, energy resilience, protection, and access to essential services. Shelter is among the top funding priorities as temperatures drop and energy infrastructure continues to face sustained attacks. The humanitarian community warns that without adequate winter response funding, families in damaged and destroyed homes — particularly in front-line regions — face severe risk. The plan was published on 22 July 2026.

Ministry of Social Policy and EIB Plan €100 Million Pilot Social Housing Programme

Ukraine's Ministry of Social Policy and the European Investment Bank are developing a €100 million pilot social housing programme, supported by €6 million in technical assistance. The programme targets IDPs and vulnerable groups and draws on the Vienna model — where 70% of residents live in social housing. The initiative is intended as a scalable blueprint for a national social housing system.

Ukraine Launches Municipal Rental Housing Fund Pilot — Affordable Rent for IDPs, Veterans and Essential Workers

Ukraine's Ministry of Development launched a pilot project to create a Municipal Rental Housing Fund, offering community-owned affordable rental housing to IDPs, veterans, families of fallen soldiers, teachers, and healthcare workers. Social Housing EIB · EU 25 Jun 2026 eib.org
EIB Commits €50 Million for Social Housing Construction Across Ukraine

The European Investment Bank announced a €50 million loan complemented by an EU grant for the construction of publicly owned, affordable, energy-efficient social and rental housing across Ukraine — targeting cities hosting large numbers of IDPs or directly affected by war. The initiative will be implemented in two phases: a pilot followed by scale-up. Announced at the Ukraine Recovery Conference in Gdańsk, the commitment brings total EIB Group support to Ukraine to €4.5 billion since February 2022. Direct programmatic relevance for organisations working on municipal housing rehabilitation under Law No. 12377 and Resolution No. 495.

Energy Resilience G7 · Energy Community 24 Jun 2026 energy-community.org
G7 Calls for €650 Million to Secure Ukraine's Energy System for Winter 2026/27

The G7+ Energy Rammstein Group has been asked to consider priority needs amounting to €650 million through the Ukraine Energy Support Fund managed by the Energy Community Secretariat. Over the past four years, G7+ countries have channelled €1.95 billion through the Fund — all fully allocated. In 2026 alone, partners contributed over €300 million. The Fund is also expanding to include additional beneficiaries from the municipal sector, particularly in district heating, water supply, and wastewater services. Swift mobilisation before winter is critical.
