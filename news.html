Social Housing EIB · EU 25 Jun 2026 eib.org
EIB Commits €50 Million for Social Housing Construction Across Ukraine

The European Investment Bank announced a €50 million loan complemented by an EU grant for the construction of publicly owned, affordable, energy-efficient social and rental housing across Ukraine — targeting cities hosting large numbers of IDPs or directly affected by war. The initiative will be implemented in two phases: a pilot followed by scale-up. Announced at the Ukraine Recovery Conference in Gdańsk, the commitment brings total EIB Group support to Ukraine to €4.5 billion since February 2022. Direct programmatic relevance for organisations working on municipal housing rehabilitation under Law No. 12377 and Resolution No. 495.

Energy Resilience G7 · Energy Community 24 Jun 2026 energy-community.org
G7 Calls for €650 Million to Secure Ukraine's Energy System for Winter 2026/27

The G7+ Energy Rammstein Group has been asked to consider priority needs amounting to €650 million through the Ukraine Energy Support Fund managed by the Energy Community Secretariat. Over the past four years, G7+ countries have channelled €1.95 billion through the Fund — all fully allocated. In 2026 alone, partners contributed over €300 million. The Fund is also expanding to include additional beneficiaries from the municipal sector, particularly in district heating, water supply, and wastewater services. Swift mobilisation before winter is critical.

Policy & Law Updates
Law 12377 2 Mar 2026
Ministry of Development & UNHCR: 70 Regulatory Acts Needed to Implement New Housing Law

A working meeting between Ukraine's Deputy Minister Kozlovska and UNHCR Representative Castel-Hollingsworth focused on implementing Law No. 12377. Once in force, the government has six months to adopt subordinate legislation — around 70 regulatory acts must be amended. The Ministry has prepared an implementation plan and is drafting the necessary documents.

CMU Resolution 4 Jun 2026
CMU Resolution #723: New Rules for IDP Housing Vouchers — Review Moves to Local Level

Resolution #723 expands grounds for refusing housing vouchers, transfers application review from oblast to municipal level, sets a 30-calendar-day review period, and introduces cross-verification between the registers of the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Ministry of Justice. Deadline for certain notarial actions: 24 July 2026.

Housing Reform Jan 2026
New Housing Law Replaces Ukraine's Soviet-Era Code — First Major Reform in 30+ Years

Law No. 12377 "On the Basic Principles of Housing Policy" — adopted in the second reading in January 2026 — replaces the 1983 Soviet Housing Code. Key provisions: social housing framework, new financial mechanisms for construction and purchase, digitalisation of processes, and priority for vulnerable groups including IDPs and persons with disabilities.
